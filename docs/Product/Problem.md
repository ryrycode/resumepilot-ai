# Problem Statement

## Background

A resume is often the first impression a candidate makes during the hiring process. Employers and Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) use resumes to determine whether an applicant possesses the qualifications, experience, and skills required for a role. However, many students, fresh graduates, and even experienced professionals struggle to create resumes that effectively communicate their value.

Although numerous online resume builders and grammar checking tools exist, many of them focus only on formatting or writing corrections. They rarely provide meaningful insights into resume quality, ATS compatibility, skill gaps, or career readiness. As a result, many applicants submit resumes that fail to highlight their strengths or meet employer expectations.

---

## Current Challenges

Many job seekers experience one or more of the following challenges:

- Difficulty organizing resume content effectively.
- Limited understanding of ATS requirements.
- Lack of awareness of missing technical or professional skills.
- Weak descriptions of work experience and projects.
- Generic resume content that fails to stand out.
- Uncertainty about how to improve their resume.
- Limited interview preparation based on their own qualifications.

These challenges can reduce confidence during job applications and may decrease the likelihood of being shortlisted for interviews.

---

## Existing Solutions

Current resume review tools often provide only partial solutions. Some focus on grammar correction, while others offer basic keyword matching or resume templates. Few platforms combine resume evaluation, ATS analysis, skill gap identification, personalized recommendations, and interview preparation into a single integrated experience.

Users are therefore required to switch between multiple tools to prepare for job applications, resulting in a fragmented and less efficient workflow.

---

## Proposed Solution

ResumePilot AI addresses these challenges by providing an AI-powered career development platform that delivers comprehensive resume analysis and personalized career guidance.

Instead of simply identifying mistakes, the platform helps users understand why improvements are necessary and provides practical recommendations tailored to their experience, skills, and target career.

By combining resume evaluation, ATS compatibility analysis, skill gap detection, interview preparation, and long-term career development, ResumePilot AI aims to become an intelligent career companion that supports users throughout their professional journey.

---

## Expected Impact

ResumePilot AI aims to help users:

- Build stronger, more competitive resumes.
- Improve ATS compatibility.
- Identify skills that should be developed.
- Prepare for interviews with greater confidence.
- Better understand employer expectations.
- Continuously improve their professional profile over time.
