# Target Users

## Overview

ResumePilot AI is designed for individuals who are preparing for employment, career advancement, or professional development. The platform serves users with varying levels of experience by providing personalized resume analysis, career guidance, and interview preparation through artificial intelligence.

The primary target users are students, fresh graduates, job seekers, career shifters, and professionals who want to strengthen their resumes and improve their employability.

---

# Primary User Groups

## 1. College Students

### Description

Students preparing for internships, on-the-job training (OJT), scholarships, leadership programs, and their first professional opportunities.

### Goals

- Create a professional resume
- Learn industry expectations
- Identify missing skills
- Prepare for internship applications
- Build confidence before graduation

### Common Challenges

- Little or no work experience
- Unsure what to include in a resume
- Limited knowledge of ATS
- Difficulty describing school projects effectively

---

## 2. Fresh Graduates

### Description

Recent graduates entering the workforce for the first time.

### Goals

- Build a competitive resume
- Increase interview opportunities
- Match resumes with specific job roles
- Understand employer expectations

### Common Challenges

- Highly competitive job market
- Limited professional experience
- Difficulty showcasing academic achievements
- Lack of interview preparation

---

## 3. Job Seekers

### Description

Individuals actively applying for new employment opportunities.

### Goals

- Improve resume quality
- Increase ATS compatibility
- Tailor resumes for specific jobs
- Receive actionable career advice

### Common Challenges

- Repeated application rejections
- Generic resumes
- Missing keywords
- Outdated resume formats

---

## 4. Career Shifters

### Description

Professionals transitioning into a different career or industry.

### Goals

- Highlight transferable skills
- Identify missing technical skills
- Understand industry expectations
- Build a resume suited for a new field

### Common Challenges

- Limited experience in the target industry
- Difficulty translating previous experience
- Skill gaps
- Employer concerns about career changes

---

## 5. Professionals

### Description

Experienced employees seeking promotions or new career opportunities.

### Goals

- Modernize resumes
- Improve personal branding
- Prepare for leadership positions
- Stay competitive in the job market

### Common Challenges

- Outdated resume content
- Poor presentation of achievements
- Missing modern technologies or certifications
- Limited understanding of current hiring trends

---

# Secondary Users

Although ResumePilot AI primarily focuses on individual users, it may also support:

- University career centers
- Guidance counselors
- Career coaches
- Professional mentors
- Training institutions

These users may use the platform to assist students or clients with resume evaluation and career preparation.

---

# User Needs

Across all user groups, ResumePilot AI aims to help users:

- Build stronger resumes
- Improve ATS compatibility
- Identify missing skills
- Receive personalized recommendations
- Prepare for interviews
- Track resume improvements over time
- Increase confidence before applying for jobs

---

# User Success

A successful user should be able to:

- Upload a resume in minutes.
- Receive meaningful AI-powered feedback.
- Understand strengths and weaknesses.
- Improve the resume using practical recommendations.
- Feel more prepared and confident before applying for jobs.
